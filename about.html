My Story

I'm Vu Hung Nguyen, an AI Solutions Engineer with over 15 years of experience in the technology industry. My journey began with a passion for solving complex problems using technology, which led me to specialize in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science.

Based in Sydney, Australia, I've had the privilege of working on cutting-edge AI projects across various industries, from retail and e-commerce to fintech and education. My expertise spans Python development, AI prompt engineering, cloud infrastructure, and building scalable AI solutions.

Beyond my professional work, I'm passionate about sharing knowledge and have been teaching Data Science and Machine Learning to help others enter this exciting field. I believe in the power of AI to transform businesses and improve lives.
